# Professional Review Checklist — v0.1

A generated project remains a draft until the professional review gate is complete.

The current checklist requires confirmation of:

- owner identity and RUT;
- exact address and georeference;
- field evidence for the electrical board/circuits;
- circuit loads and conductor verification;
- zero unresolved compliance blockers;
- presence of electrical plan, unilinear, load schedule and symbol legend.

This checklist is intentionally separate from the SEC declaration action.

For Casa Goyo, current evidence is enough to establish the board baseline but not enough to mark the TE1 package review-complete.
