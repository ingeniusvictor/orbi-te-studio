# Minimum Remote Field Package — Draft v0.1

This checklist is intentionally conservative. Its goal is to let a remote technician collect enough evidence for ORBI TE Studio to prepare a project without fabricating missing facts.

## A. Property
- owner full name and RUT
- exact address
- commune and region
- destination/use
- geolocation from phone
- clear exterior/front photo

## B. Existing electrical board
- straight frontal photo with door open
- close-up of every protection label
- photo with circuit legend visible
- interior photo only when safe and performed by qualified personnel
- main supply / meter identification

## C. Installation
- architectural plan, old electrical plan or measured sketch
- room names and approximate dimensions
- lighting points per room
- outlet points per room
- kitchen/loggia dedicated loads
- fixed electrical loads and nameplates

## D. Verification measurements
Values must come from actual instruments and cannot be inferred from images.
- protective conductor continuity
- insulation resistance
- RCD functional/test evidence as applicable
- earthing measurements as applicable
- supply voltage
- other checks required by the applicable verified RIC rules

## E. Photo quality gate
ORBI should request a retake when:
- rating text is unreadable
- device is partly hidden
- perspective prevents reliable identification
- circuit numbering cannot be matched
- nameplate is overexposed or blurred

## Output states
Every extracted field is tagged as:
- OBSERVED
- REPORTED
- CALCULATED
- VERIFIED
- PENDING
- CONFLICT

No PENDING or CONFLICT field that is required for compliance may be silently replaced by an assumption.
